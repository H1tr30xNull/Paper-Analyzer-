# Paper Analysis Report Template

Prose in the user's language; titles, terms, and quotes in the original.
Mark author claims as *"Authors claim…"* and analysis conclusions as **Assessment:**.

```markdown
# Paper Analysis: <title>

> **Authors / Venue / Year:** … · **Link:** … · **Code:** yes/no (link)
> **Reader goal:** Understanding | Lit review | Implementation | Reproduction | Review | Gap discovery | New direction
> **Novelty:** x/10 · **Overall evidence:** Strong | Moderate | Weak

## 1. Executive Summary
(≤ 1 page) Problem, core idea, main result, how much to trust it, where it sits, the single best next move.

## 2. Contribution Summary
| # | Contribution (authors' claim) | Assessment |
|---|---|---|

## 3. Evidence Assessment
| Claim | Evidence (exp / fig / table / metric / stats) | Rating | Note |
|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Novelty Analysis
- **Truly novel:** …
- **Incremental:** …
- **Reused components:** … (origin: …)
- **Missing citations:** … (impact on novelty claim: …)
- **Novelty score:** x/10 — justification, measured against: …

## 5. Related Work Positioning
| Paper | Year | Relation (foundation / competitor / SOTA / follow-up) | Cited? | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
Short paragraph: where this paper sits in the field and what has happened since.

## 6. Critical Review
**Strengths:** …
**Weaknesses:**
- Hidden assumptions: …
- Failure modes: …
- Benchmark biases: …
- Reproducibility risks: …
- Missing experiments: …
- Unfair comparisons: …

## 7. Research Opportunities
| # | Opportunity | Type | Novelty | Impact | Feasibility | Pub. potential | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
For the top 1–3: what to do, why it's open, first experiment.

## Appendix: Extraction
Problem · Motivation · Assumptions · Methodology · Experiments · Results

## Sources
Numbered list of related papers and links used.
```
